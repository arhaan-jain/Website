Beyond research

Building things that help people.

Alongside robotics research, I've worked on assistive devices, manufacturing automation, and community projects. These experiences shape how I approach engineering: build carefully, test with people, and understand the setting in which a system will be used.

PillBuddy

Medical Grand Challenge · 2024

Designed and built an automated pill-dispenser prototype with an interdisciplinary team of six students in computing, medicine, and engineering. Used Arduino, 3D printing, and machining to develop the hardware, with the aim of supporting medication management for people taking multiple medicines.

The team was a Nascent Category finalist in NUS's Medical Grand Challenge 2024.
